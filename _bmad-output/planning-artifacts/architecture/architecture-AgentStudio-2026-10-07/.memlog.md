- (decision) REQUIRED DURABILITY SEMANTICS (coach-defined, for candidate evaluation): (1) RESUMABLE EPISODES - an episode suspended awaiting an external input resumes days later with state, evidence, specification version and context intact; (2) RETRIES - transient failures retried without duplicating effects; (3) RECOVERY AFTER PROCESS FAILURE - no lost writes, an episode interrupted mid-turn recovers to a consistent state; (4) IDEMPOTENT SIDE EFFECTS - exactly-once external effects, or at-least-once with an idempotency key the adapter honours; (5) DURABLE TOOL EXECUTION - a tool call in flight when the process dies is completed or safely retried, never silently lost; (6) EVIDENCE CONSISTENCY - the evidence record and the effect it describes cannot diverge; (7) CROSS-SESSION CONTINUATION - resumption triggered by an inbound message, not only a timer.

If it constrains, validates, authorizes, persists, observes or executes, it belongs in the platform.
