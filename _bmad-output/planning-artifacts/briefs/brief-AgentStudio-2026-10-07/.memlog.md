- (decision) COACH PROPOSAL (to be confirmed) - THE PATTERN: a stranger contacts a small business wanting either something it KNOWS or something it can DO, and the episode must end in one of a small FIXED set of dispositions: answered | action taken | escalated | out of scope. - (decision) COACH DESIGN (leading, as instructed) - THE CONSTRAINED OUTCOME MODEL: invariant ENVELOPE + business-defined PAYLOAD. Envelope resolution field takes one of FIVE business-neutral kinds: fulfilled (agent completed what was asked) | committed (bounded action taken or commitment recorded, with terms) | informed (authoritative answer given, nothing further needed) | deferred (handed to a human, with reason) | refused (out of scope, ineligible or policy-blocked, with reason). Also invariant: which declared goal, which required information obtained, which tool contracts fired and returned what, grounding citations for every factual claim, episode identity. PAYLOAD is business-defined structured fields. This split is what lets EVALUATION live in the reusable layer: invariant assertions hold everywhere while business criteria assert over the payload. Validated against all four example requests.

- (decision) COACH DESIGN - NOT-AN-FAQ-BOT DISCRIMINATOR is OBLIGATION: the agent knows what must be true before a request can be resolved and will not resolve until obligations are met or explicitly waived. - (insight) SHARPEST NEW FINDING (garden centre): inferring a CAUSE and determining FAULT are different acts with different consequences.
