Direction 4 — Almanac

The personality is the considered reference work: old-style serif headings over a humanist sans body, a wide measure and a very open vertical rhythm, with borders doing almost all the structural work and depth reduced to a single near-invisible lift. Mixed type families carry the hierarchy outright, which is how five sections stay unmistakable peers without any chrome. The trade-off it accepts: it is the airiest of the four, so an operator scrolls more to see their whole business, and the serif warmth risks reading as editorial rather than operational on a day when something has gone wrong.
