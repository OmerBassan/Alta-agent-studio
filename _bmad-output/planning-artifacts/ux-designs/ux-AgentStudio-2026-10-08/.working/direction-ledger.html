Direction 1 — Ledger

The personality is the printed record: an editorial serif on warm paper, hairline rules in place of cards, and no shadow anywhere. It earns trust by looking like something already written down and kept, which makes the Understanding read as the operator's own document rather than an application's output. The trade-off it accepts: at this reading size less fits on screen than in a denser direction, and its deliberate quietness leaves little visual headroom for a moment that genuinely needs to claim attention.
