Direction 2 — Quiet Office

The personality is the modern working surface that stays out of the way: a neutral grotesque sans, cool greys, one soft elevation step and a single 10px radius, set to a tighter rhythm so more of the business is legible at once. It reads as competent software a business already trusts with its day. The trade-off it accepts: the register is familiar enough to be near-invisible, so it carries less of the product's own character than a more opinionated direction, and the panelled sections edge closer to looking like an application surface than a document.
