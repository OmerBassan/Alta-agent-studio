Direction 3 — Register

The personality is the plain institutional record: a wide-set humanist sans at a compact rhythm, square corners, firm rules and high weight contrast, with no shadow anywhere except a single lift under a moment that asks for a decision. It is built to be read fast and not misread, which is why boundaries here look structural rather than decorative. The trade-off it accepts: the density and the hard edges read as serious rather than warm, and the direction has to work harder than the others to feel conversational rather than administrative.
