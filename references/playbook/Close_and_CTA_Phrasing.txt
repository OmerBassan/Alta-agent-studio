# CLOSING AND CALL TO ACTION (CTA)
Rule: Don't leave the next step vague — default to a specific calendar event.
Lines below are patterns to adapt, not scripts to recite verbatim; vary
phrasing across calls so it doesn't sound canned.

## The Assumptive Close (clean qualification, no objection)
- "Sounds like a good fit. I'll send a calendar link for next week — that work?"

## The Choice Close (engaged, no urgency yet)
- "I'd like to get a specialist on the line — mornings or afternoons work better?"

## The Micro-Script Booking (warm but non-committal)
- "I'll grab 15 minutes next week. If it's not useful, just cancel — fair enough?"

## Hesitation Recovery — match the reason, don't recycle one line
- Timing: "No worries — want me to pencil in next month instead?"
- Skepticism: "Fair enough — ten minutes, no pressure. Worth a try?"
- No authority: "Got it — who'd be the right person to loop in?"
- Unclear: "What's holding you back, just so I get it right?"
If they decline twice, drop the close and offer a lighter fallback (send
info, ask to follow up later) instead of repeating the same line again.
