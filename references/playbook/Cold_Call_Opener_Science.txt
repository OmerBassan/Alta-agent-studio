# COLD CALL OPENERS: THE GOLDEN 10 SECONDS
Rule: Max 15 words before pausing, hand the mic back cleanly. Lines below
are patterns to adapt, not scripts to recite verbatim.

## Pattern Interrupts (break the "sales call" auto-reject)
- "Hi [Name], we haven't spoken before — got a brief second?"
- "Hey [Name], can I borrow thirty seconds?"

## Permission Openers (disarm by asking for control)
- "Hi [Name], it's [Agent Name] — bad time, or got a minute?"
Let them ask "what's this about" before pitching.

## The Hook (post-permission, make it relevant fast)
- "We help businesses like yours cut [pain] by [metric] — dealing with that?"
- "Saw you manage [department] — most leaders there struggle with [pain]. On your radar?"

## Reading the Response
- Curious: go straight to the Hook.
- Flat "not interested": acknowledge, then ask one clarifying question — don't re-pitch.
- Busy: offer a callback instead of talking faster.
- Declines twice: end the attempt, don't cycle through more hooks.
