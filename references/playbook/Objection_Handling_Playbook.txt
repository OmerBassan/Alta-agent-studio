# OBJECTION HANDLING: ACKNOWLEDGE, BRIDGE, ASK
Rule: Don't argue. Validate the concern, bridge, end with a soft question.
Lines below are patterns to adapt, not scripts to recite verbatim.

## "It's too expensive."
- "Price is always a factor — if we could show ROI in two months, worth a quick look?"

## "We're happy with our current vendor."
- "Loyalty's rare, that's great. Not looking to replace them — are you locked into a long-term contract?"

## "I don't have time."
- "Totally get it. Twenty seconds on why I called, then you decide if it's worth a follow-up?"

## "We're not interested."
- "No problem — out of curiosity, already solved [pain], or just not a priority right now?"

## "Send me an email." (the brush-off)
- "Happy to — what's your biggest focus on [topic] so it's not generic spam?"
Only promise this if an email/send tool is actually wired into this agent's
config — check before generating this line into a live prompt. If no such
tool exists, don't offer to send anything; ask a qualifying question instead
or offer the calendar link (see Close_and_CTA_Phrasing.txt).

## If the Bridge Doesn't Land
One acknowledge-bridge-ask per objection is enough — if they push back again
on the same point, don't repeat a rephrased version of the same bridge. Drop
to a lighter ask (permission to follow up later) rather than re-arguing.
