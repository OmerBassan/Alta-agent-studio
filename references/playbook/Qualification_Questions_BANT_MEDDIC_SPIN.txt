# VOICE-FIRST QUALIFICATION (BANT, MEDDIC, SPIN)
Rule: one question per turn, max 20 words, contractions, conversational —
not an interrogation. Lines below are patterns to adapt, not a fixed script
to run in order every call.

BANT is this platform's primary qualification framework (see project scope).
Use it as the backbone. MEDDIC/SPIN questions below are optional deepening —
pull one in only if BANT alone doesn't give the agent/booking flow enough to
act on, not as four parallel checklists to work through on every call.

## BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)
- Need: "What's the biggest headache with [current process]?"
- Timeline: "When are you hoping to have this solved by?"
- Authority: "Who else usually weighs in on a decision like this?"
- Budget: "Rough budget carved out for this, or still exploring?"

## MEDDIC (optional, if BANT leaves a gap)
- Pain: "What happens if you keep doing things the way you are now?"
- Champion: "If you like what you see, who actually signs off?"

## SPIN (optional, if the lead needs to talk through the problem first)
- Situation: "How are you handling [process] today?"
- Problem: "Where does that usually break down?"
- Implication: "When it breaks down, how much time does that cost you?"
- Need-Payoff: "If that step were automated, what would you focus on instead?"

## Working the List
- Don't re-ask something the lead already told you earlier in the call —
  check what's already known before asking, not just the fixed order above.
- If they decline or dodge a question (budget is the common one), don't
  press twice — note it as unknown and move on; a missing field isn't a
  disqualification.
- Skip straight to whichever question matters most for THIS lead's answers
  so far, rather than working the list top to bottom regardless of context.
