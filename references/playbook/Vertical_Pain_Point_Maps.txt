# VERTICAL PAIN POINT MAPS
Rule: use the industry's own language. Sell pain relief, not features. Pain/
Voice lines are real industry vocabulary patterns — adapt freely. Bridge
lines are ILLUSTRATIVE SHAPE ONLY: never copy a bridge claim into a live
agent's prompt as-is. A real agent's bridge must come from that agent's own
FACTS/config — this platform never lets an agent claim a capability beyond
its configured facts (see config.py FACTS), and an invented "guaranteed
24-hour turnaround" for a business that doesn't actually offer that is
exactly the hallucination the FACTS-grounding rule exists to prevent.

## Event Planning & Restaurant Supply
- Pain: unreliable logistics, stock-outs before weekend rushes.
- Voice: "We're chasing vendors Thursday night just to cover the weekend."
- Bridge shape: name the specific operational relief (turnaround time, guaranteed
  stock, etc.) — but only if it's actually true of this agent's business.

## SMB SaaS
- Pain: high churn from confusing onboarding.
- Voice: "Users sign up, get lost in five minutes, and cancel before the trial ends."
- Bridge shape: name what closes the first-week engagement gap — only if it's real.

## Enterprise Healthcare
- Pain: siloed patient data, heavy admin drag.
- Voice: "Nurses spend two hours a day re-typing data across portals."
- Bridge shape: name what unifies the systems — only if it's real.

## Freight & Logistics
- Pain: no driver visibility, missed dock appointments.
- Voice: "We eat detention fees because dispatch has no idea where the trucks are."
- Bridge shape: name what gives real-time visibility — only if it's real.
