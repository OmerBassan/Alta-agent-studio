# The taproom for private events

Hopfield Brewery's taproom hosts private events for 10 to 60 guests. Events can start at 12:00, 15:00 or 18:00 and run for up to four hours.

# Hire fees

Hiring the taproom costs 400 dollars for a daytime event and 650 dollars for an evening event starting at 18:00. A 25 percent deposit secures the date. Food and drink are charged on top.

# Food and drink

We offer three packages: Pints and Pretzels at 18 dollars per guest, the Brewer's Table at 32 dollars per guest, and a full Tap Takeover at 45 dollars per guest. All packages include a soft-drink option.

# Holds and deposits

We can hold a date for 7 days while you decide. A hold costs nothing. The date is confirmed only when the deposit is paid. Deposits are refundable up to 30 days before the event.

# Age policy

Everyone drinking alcohol must be 21 or over, and photo ID is checked at the door. Guests under 21 are welcome at daytime events with a guardian.
