# Opening hours

Bright Smile Dental is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00, and Saturday 9:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

# Services and fees

A check-up and clean costs 95 dollars and takes 45 minutes. A filling consultation costs 60 dollars. A root canal consultation costs 120 dollars; the treatment itself is quoted by the dentist after the consultation, because it depends on the tooth.

# What happens at a root canal consultation

At a root canal consultation the dentist examines the tooth, takes an X-ray and explains whether treatment is advised and what it would involve. Nothing is treated at the consultation itself. Treatment, if advised, is usually one or two appointments of about 90 minutes each, under local anaesthetic.

# Booking and cancellation

Appointments can be booked for check-ups, filling consultations and root canal consultations. Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel; late cancellations may be charged 30 dollars.

# Urgent problems

If you have swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, bleeding that will not stop, or a knocked-out tooth, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. For severe toothache during opening hours, call the practice on 555-0142.
