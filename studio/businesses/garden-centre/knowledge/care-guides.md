# Watering plants in pots

Plants in pots dry out faster than plants in the ground, but most Mediterranean plants in pots need water only when the top few centimetres of compost are dry. Make sure the pot drains freely.

# Lavender

Lavender likes full sun, poor free-draining soil and little water once established. Trim lightly after flowering.

# Roses

Water roses deeply once or twice a week in dry spells and feed in spring. Black spots on leaves are a fungal problem; remove affected leaves.

# Planting hardy shrubs

Dig a hole twice the width of the pot, water the plant well before planting and keep it watered through its first summer.
