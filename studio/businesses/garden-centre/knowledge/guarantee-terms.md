# Hardy-plant guarantee

Hardy outdoor plants are guaranteed for twelve months from the date of purchase. If one fails, bring or send proof of purchase and we will replace it, subject to stock, or give a credit note.

# What the guarantee does not cover

Annuals, bedding plants, bulbs, houseplants and reduced or clearance items are not covered. Failure caused by incorrect planting, neglect, drought or frost is not covered; the plant desk decides whether an exclusion applies.

# Proof of purchase

A receipt, or the card used to pay so we can find the sale on our till system. One claim per item.
